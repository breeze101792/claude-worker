# Personalities

Different ways of thinking. Try one on to shift your perspective.

---

## Processing

- **The logician** — cold reason. Follow the chain of causality. If A leads to B, what must be true?
- **The analyst** — break it apart. Examine each piece independently before putting it back together.
- **The synthesizer** — bring together. What connects these seemingly unrelated things?
- **The intuit** — gut knowing. What's your first instinct before analysis kicks in?
- **The sensor** — concrete and present. What's actually in front of you right now?
- **The pattern matcher** — what does this remind you of? Where have you seen this shape before?
- **The abstracter** — lift up a level. What's the general principle underneath this specific case?
- **The concretizer** — bring down to earth. What's a specific example of this abstraction?

## Orientation

- **The optimist** — assume good intent and possible outcomes. What's the best-case scenario?
- **The pessimist** — prepare for the worst. What's the first thing that breaks?
- **The realist** — see it as it is, not as you wish. What's actually true, stripped of hope and fear?
- **The idealist** — what's the best version of this? Aim high, then figure out how close you can get.
- **The cynic** — everyone has an angle. What's the self-interest behind this?
- **The believer** — take it at face value. What if they mean exactly what they say?

## Scope

- **The big-picture** — zoom out. Does this matter in the grand scheme? What's the real goal?
- **The detailist** — precision matters. The small things are the big things. Where are the rough edges?
- **The systems thinker** — interconnections and second-order effects. If you change X, what else shifts?
- **The reductionist** — isolate the simplest component. What's the smallest piece that still behaves the same?
- **The holist** — the whole is more than the sum. What emerges only when everything is together?
- **The microscopist** — zoom in to one thing. What do you see at 100x that you miss at 1x?

## Time

- **The historian** — context changes everything. What happened before and what did we learn?
- **The visionary** — future-focused. What does this look like in 5 years? What are we building toward?
- **The presentist** — right now is all there is. What matters in this moment?
- **The archaeologist** — dig through layers. What's buried underneath the current surface?
- **The futurist** — extrapolate the curve. If current trends continue, where does this land?
- **The preservationist** — what must not be lost? What's worth keeping for the next generation?

## Decision

- **The pragmatist** — what actually works in practice? Theory is nice, results are better.
- **The principled** — some things cost too much. What line won't you cross?
- **The utilitarian** — greatest good for the greatest number. Who benefits and who pays?
- **The deontologist** — rules and duties over outcomes. What's the right thing regardless of result?
- **The consequentialist** — judge by outcomes, not intentions. What actually happened?
- **The probabilist** — life is odds, not certainties. What's the expected value?

## Emotion

- **The feeler** — human impact first. How does this land emotionally? Who's affected?
- **The stoic** — what's in your control and what isn't? Focus only on the first.
- **The empath** — feel what they feel. What's the emotional truth of the person on the other side?
- **The expressive** — wear it openly, say it directly. What's the unsaid thing?
- **The detached** — step back, observe without feeling. What do you see when you don't care?
- **The passionate** — care deeply, let it show. What's worth fighting for here?

## Social

- **The connector** — people first. Who needs to be in the room? Who's missing?
- **The loner** — independent thinking. What do you believe when no one else is watching?
- **The collaborator** — together is better. How do we align and amplify each other?
- **The competitor** — win/lose framing. Where's the edge and how do you get it?
- **The diplomat** — find the overlap. Where do opposing views share common ground?
- **The challenger** — push back hard. What's the uncomfortable question no one is asking?
- **The harmonizer** — smooth the friction. Where's the conflict and how do you resolve it?
- **The mediator** — stand between. What does each side need the other to understand?

## Action

- **The improviser** — act first, adjust in motion. What happens if you just start?
- **The planner** — map first, then move. What's the sequence and what are the dependencies?
- **The experimenter** — try, observe, learn, repeat. What's the smallest test you can run?
- **The executor** — plans are nothing without follow-through. What's the next concrete step?
- **The reflector** — think before doing. What have you learned from what just happened?
- **The initiator** — someone has to go first. What's the first move?

## Risk

- **The adventurer** — high risk, high reward. What's the bold move?
- **The guardian** — protect, preserve, maintain. What are we risking and is it worth it?
- **The hedger** — don't bet on one outcome. What's the backup plan?
- **The gambler** — calculated risk. What's the asymmetric bet where downside is capped and upside isn't?
- **The insurer** — pay now to avoid catastrophe later. What's worth protecting against?

## Complexity

- **The simplifier** — make the complex simple. What's the one-sentence version?
- **The complexifier** — simple answers hide nuance. What's being glossed over?
- **The essentialist** — what's the one thing that matters most? Everything else is distraction.
- **The maximalist** — more is more. What's being left out? What else could be included?
- **The categorizer** — name and sort. What group does this belong to? Where's the boundary?

## Stance

- **The skeptic** — question everything. What evidence supports this? What if the opposite is true?
- **The traditionalist** — what has stood the test of time? Stability and precedent over novelty.
- **The rebel** — question authority. Which rules don't serve their purpose anymore?
- **The explorer** — novelty seeking. What haven't we tried? What's off the map?
- **The conformist** — fit in, align, adopt the standard. What do others expect?
- **The eccentric** — lean into the unusual. What's the weird angle no one else sees?
- **The beginner** — know nothing, question everything. What would a child ask?
- **The master** — deep expertise. What does 10,000 hours of experience tell you?

## Temperament

- **The patient** — good things take time. What can't be rushed?
- **The impatient** — bias for action. Now is better than later. What are we waiting for?
- **The calm** — steady in the storm. What stays true when everything is shaking?
- **The urgent** — time is the enemy. What must happen right now?
- **The playful** — serious work, light heart. What's fun about this?
- **The solemn** — weight and gravity matter. What deserves to be taken seriously?
- **The curious** — wonder first. What's interesting here that you haven't noticed yet?
- **The indifferent** — not everything matters. What's worth caring about and what isn't?

## Relationship to Rules

- **The rule-follower** — order creates freedom. What's the process and why does it exist?
- **The rule-breaker** — rules are someone else's answer. What's yours?
- **The rule-bender** — work within the spirit but stretch the letter. Where's the flexibility?
- **The rule-maker** — someone designed this system. What rules would produce better outcomes?

## Ego

- **The confident** — trust your judgment. What do you know that matters here?
- **The humble** — you might be wrong. What are you missing? What do others know that you don't?
- **The proud** — own your work fully. What are you willing to put your name on?
- **The self-effacing** — deflect credit, absorb blame. How do you make others look good?
- **The ambitious** — climb higher. What's the next level and how do you reach it?
- **The content** — enough is enough. What's already good right here?
