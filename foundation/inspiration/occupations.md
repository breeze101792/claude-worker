# Occupations

Different professional lenses. Ask: how would a ___ approach this?

---

## Science & Medicine

- **Doctor** — diagnose before treating. What are the symptoms? What's the root cause?
- **Surgeon** — cut with precision and confidence. What must be removed and what must be preserved?
- **Nurse** — care for the whole person, not just the condition. What makes this bearable?
- **Paramedic** — stabilize first, act fast. What keeps this alive until it reaches help?
- **Psychologist** — what's beneath the surface? What pattern is repeating?
- **Therapist** — hold space without judgment. What needs to be said but hasn't been?
- **Researcher** — form a hypothesis, test it. What would prove this wrong?
- **Epidemiologist** — trace the spread. Where did this start and how does it move?
- **Pharmacist** — interactions and side effects. What else is in the system?

## Engineering & Tech

- **Software engineer** — build it clean, maintainable, correct. What's the minimal change?
- **Mechanical engineer** — forces, materials, tolerances. What's under stress?
- **Electrical engineer** — current, resistance, signal. Where does the circuit break?
- **Civil engineer** — it must stand for decades. What happens under extreme load?
- **Sysadmin** — keep it running. What fails at 3am?
- **DevOps/SRE** — reliability is a feature. What's the blast radius?
- **QA tester** — break it on purpose. What input should never happen but will?
- **Data scientist** — what does the data actually say, not what you want it to say?
- **Security analyst** — find the door someone left open. What's the weakest link?
- **Product manager** — what's worth building and why? Who needs this?

## Architecture & Design

- **Architect** — space shapes behavior. How does this feel to move through?
- **Urban planner** — city as a system. How do people, traffic, and time intersect?
- **Interior designer** — light, texture, flow. What mood does this room create?
- **Graphic designer** — hierarchy, contrast, clarity. Where does the eye go first?
- **Industrial designer** — form meets function in the hand. How does it feel to use?
- **Fashion designer** — identity expressed through material. What story does this tell?
- **Game designer** — rules create fun. What makes this satisfying to learn?

## Building & Trades

- **Carpenter** — measure twice, cut once. What can't be undone?
- **Electrician** — power is invisible and deadly. What's still live?
- **Plumber** — water finds every leak. Where's the weak seal?
- **Welder** — two becomes one with heat. Is the bond strong enough?
- **Mason** — stone by stone, level by level. Is the foundation true?
- **Mechanic** — listen to the engine. What sound doesn't belong?
- **Roofer** — water runs downhill. Where does it pool?
- **Locksmith** — every lock is a puzzle. What's the simplest way through?
- **Machinist** — thousandths of an inch matter. What's the tolerance?

## Business & Finance

- **CEO** — vision, people, capital. Where are we going and why?
- **Accountant** — every number tells a story. What doesn't add up?
- **Auditor** — trust but verify. What's being hidden in plain sight?
- **Banker** — risk and return. What's the downside?
- **Investor** — bet on the future. What's undervalued?
- **Actuary** — quantify the unknown. What are the real odds?
- **Management consultant** — see the pattern others miss. What would you change if you could start over?
- **Negotiator** — find the overlap of interests. What do they actually want?

## Law & Government

- **Lawyer** — argue from evidence and precedent. What does the text actually say?
- **Judge** — hear both sides, stay impartial. What's fair, not who's louder?
- **Police officer** — observe, de-escalate, protect. What's actually happening here?
- **Detective** — follow the evidence, not the story. Who benefits?
- **Firefighter** — contain before extinguishing. What's feeding the fire?
- **Diplomat** — find common ground. Where do opposing views overlap?
- **Social worker** — meet people where they are. What support is missing?
- **Spy** — gather without being noticed. What's the signal in the noise?

## Arts & Entertainment

- **Actor** — inhabit the role. What does this character believe?
- **Director** — every frame is a choice. What's the audience feeling right now?
- **Screenwriter** — structure, conflict, payoff. What's the question the story asks?
- **Musician** — rhythm, melody, silence. What's the space between the notes?
- **Painter** — color, light, composition. What do you leave out?
- **Photographer** — frame the moment. What's in the frame and what's excluded?
- **Novelist** — voice, pace, detail. Whose eyes are we seeing through?
- **Journalist** — who, what, when, where, why. What's the truth and who's shaping it?
- **Editor** — cut what doesn't serve the whole. What's essential?
- **Comedian** — timing is everything. Where's the unexpected truth?
- **Curator** — selection is meaning. What belongs together?

## Education

- **Teacher** — meet the student where they are. What's the simplest way in?
- **Professor** — depth over breadth. What framework makes this make sense?
- **Librarian** — organize so others can find. What system of retrieval works?
- **Coach** — see the potential, build the habit. What's one thing to improve today?
- **Historian** — context changes everything. What was happening at the time?

## Service & Hospitality

- **Chef** — ingredients, process, timing. What goes in, in what order, for how long?
- **Bartender** — read the room. What does this person actually need?
- **Flight attendant** — calm in a metal tube at 35,000 feet. What makes people feel safe?
- **Pilot** — checklists, instruments, judgment. What's the backup plan?
- **Concierge** — know the hidden doors. What's the real ask?
- **Waiter** — anticipate before being called. What's about to be needed?

## Agriculture & Nature

- **Farmer** — work with seasons, not against them. What can't be rushed?
- **Gardener** — plant, water, wait. What needs time and what needs pruning?
- **Fisherman** — patience and the right spot. Are you where the fish are?
- **Beekeeper** — small actions, collective outcome. How does each part serve the whole?
- **Forester** — think in decades. What does this look like in 50 years?
- **Ecologist** — everything is connected. If you pull here, what moves over there?

## Sports & Fitness

- **Athlete** — train, perform, recover. Is this sustainable and repeatable?
- **Referee** — enforce the rules fairly. What's the standard, not the emotion?
- **Personal trainer** — meet them at their edge. What's the next achievable goal?
- **Physical therapist** — rebuild stronger. What's weak and how do you strengthen it?
- **Nutritionist** — input shapes output. What's being consumed?

## Religion & Philosophy

- **Philosopher** — question the premise. What are we really asking?
- **Priest / Rabbi / Imam** — hold the sacred. What matters beyond the material?
- **Monk** — silence and repetition reveal truth. What do you hear when you stop?
- **Chaplain** — presence before answers. What comfort is needed right now?
- **Ethicist** — what should we do, not just what can we do? Who's affected?

## Unique & Extreme

- **Astronaut** — in a void, systems are everything. What happens if this fails up there?
- **Deep-sea diver** — hostile environment, full reliance on gear. What's your air supply?
- **Bomb disposal** — one wrong move. Where's the margin for error?
- **Search and rescue** — time is the enemy. Where would you be if you were lost?
- **Funeral director** — honor the end with dignity. What matters when everything is done?
- **Tattoo artist** — permanent mark, no undo. Are you sure this is right?
- **Translator** — meaning across worlds. What's lost between languages?
- **Bodyguard** — threat before it manifests. What's out of place?
- **Mountain guide** — respect the mountain. When do you turn back?
